# **Childcare Programme: Streamlined Instalment Payment SOP**

**Programme:** Professional Childcare & Early Years Programme (15 Weeks)

**Policy:** 2-Part Instalment Plan (Pre-Start Requirement). Both instalments must clear before Cohort Day 1 to receive LMS access.

### **Instalment Payment Process & Email Sequence**

#### **Step 1: First Instalment Paid (Deposit)**

* **Learner Action:** Learner pays Instalment 1 (₦40,000 / $37.50) via the portal.  
* **System Action:** CRM updates status to *"Provisional \- Instalment 1 Paid"* and triggers **Email 1** immediately.

Plaintext

```

[EMAIL PROMPT 1: Deposit Confirmation & Due Date]

Subject: Deposit Received! Next Instalment Due Date for Childcare Programme

Dear [Learner Name],

Thank you for securing your spot in the Professional Childcare & Early Years Programme!

We have successfully received your first instalment payment of [Amount Paid: ₦40,000 / $37.50].

YOUR PAYMENT SUMMARY & NEXT DUE DATE:
• First Instalment Paid: [Amount Paid]
• Second Instalment Outstanding: [Remaining Balance: ₦40,000 / $37.50]
• Due Date for Second Instalment: [Due Date Date]

Please note that both instalments must be completed before Day 1 to unlock full learning platform access.

You can complete your second instalment at any time before the due date using the link below:
[Link: Complete Second Instalment]

If you have any questions, feel free to reply directly to this email.

Warm regards,  
The Oakvale Learning Team

```

#### **Step 2: Balance Reminder (7 Days Out)**

* **Learner Action:** None required yet.  
* **System Action:** System identifies unpaid balance 7 days prior to the due date and triggers **Email 2** automatically.

Plaintext

```

[EMAIL PROMPT 2: Payment Reminder (7 Days Before Due Date)]

Subject: Reminder: Your 2nd Instalment is Due in 7 Days

Dear [Learner Name],

This is a quick reminder that the second and final instalment for your Professional Childcare & Early Years Programme is due in 7 days ([Due Date Date]).

BALANCE DETAILS:
• Outstanding Balance: [Remaining Balance: ₦40,000 / $37.50]
• Payment Deadline: [Due Date Date]

To ensure your enrolment is confirmed and your access to the learning portal is unlocked on time for Cohort Day 1, please complete your payment using the link below:

[Link: Pay 2nd Instalment Now]

If you have already made this payment, please disregard this email.

Best regards,  
The Oakvale Learning Team

```

#### 

#### **Step 3: Second Instalment Paid (Enrolment & Onboarding)**

* **Learner Action:** Learner pays Instalment 2 (₦40,000 / $37.50).  
* **System Action:** CRM updates status to *"Fully Paid \- Enrolled"* and triggers **Email 3** immediately.

```

• LMS Link: [Link: Access Free Community Platform]
```
